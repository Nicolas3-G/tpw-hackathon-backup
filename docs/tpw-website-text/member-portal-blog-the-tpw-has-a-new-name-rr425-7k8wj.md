# AB 581 (Carrillo)

Sep 10

Written by [Kristy Dinsmoor](https://www.thetpw.org/)

Assembly Bill 581 went into effect in January 2024. We understand that implementing these new procedures has been challenging, and we are committed to working closely with our TPW members and CDCR to ensure that every institution aligns with the new law.

To help with this transition, TPW is co-writing a FAQ for AB 581. Our goal is to clarify the new processes and address any issues that arise during implementation. We’ll share it with you as soon as it is complete and approved.

If you encounter any difficulties with obtaining gate clearances or navigating the new procedures, please fill out [this form](https://forms.gle/MWWSYWiiUs9ZqbD29).

Thank you for your continued support and communication! Together, we can make sure AB 581 is successfully implemented.

[Kristy Dinsmoor](https://www.thetpw.org/)
