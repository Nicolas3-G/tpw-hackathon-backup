# Member Contributions

Our mission, to dismantle silos, foster meaningful exchanges among individuals and organizations that share common values, and increase resources and access for Community-Based Organizations (CBOs), was born because **we believe in you**. Our purpose is to help you do what you do best. If you believe in our mission, have found value in the spaces or support we offer, and are in a position to contribute, consider supporting Transformative Programming Works. 

We are restructuring and simplifying our *voluntary dues* sliding scale to better reflect TPW's costs. **We are asking all of our members to contribute 1% of the funding they receive from the RIGHT grant.**  CDCR has confirmed that funds allocated in your admin budget line can be used toward TPW membership dues. 

Your support will enable us to continue advocating for you and pursuing our collective objective of delivering high-quality, community-led programs to every individual incarcerated in California prisons.

Thank you for being part of this community and for everything you continue to do.
