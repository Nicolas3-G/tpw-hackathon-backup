# [For the Win: CBO PROVIDED PROGRAMS CUT CDCR RECIDIVISM RATE IN HALF](https://www.thetpw.org/blog-post-title-one-dcwrn)

In February 2024, the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation (CDCR) released their [*latest recidivism report*](https://www.cdcr.ca.gov/research/wp-content/uploads/sites/174/2024/02/Statewide-Recidivism-Report-for-Individuals-Released-in-Fiscal-Year-2018-19.pdf), indicating a decline in recidivism rates. This positive trend is attributed by CDCR to the effects of recent criminal legal reforms, including the enactment of Proposition 57, which has played a critical role in expanding rehabilitative programming in the prison system, highlighting the connection between comprehensive rehabilitation services and reduced recidivism.
