# Save the date!

Dec 13

Written by [Gabriela Garcia](https://www.thetpw.org/)

**Date:** 04/21/24 - 04/23/24\
**Location:** Sacramento, CA

The TPW Annual Member Convening is back, and we’re bringing together community-based organizations, CDCR staff, legislators, and advocates for three days of connection, collaboration, and action.

**Mark your calendars for this exciting 3-day event:**

- **Day 1: Mixer & Networking Reception\**
  Kick off the convening with an informal networking mixer where you can meet fellow advocates, CDCR staff, and legislators. Enjoy light refreshments and start building meaningful connections.

- **Day 2: Convening Day\**
  A full day of dynamic workshops, presentations, and discussions. Breakout rooms will host multiple sessions simultaneously, offering in-depth exploration of topics ranging from prison programming to advocacy strategies. Members will have the opportunity to lead presentations and share their expertise, while the TPW team unveils our vision for the year ahead, energizing everyone for upcoming advocacy efforts.

- **Day 3: Advocacy Day at the State Capitol\**
  Join us for a day of advocacy at the State Capitol, where we’ll take our collective voice directly to policymakers. This is your chance to engage with legislators and champion the issues that matter most to our community!

The TPW Annual Convening is not just an event—it’s an opportunity to strengthen our movement, share resources, and amplify our collective impact. Get ready to learn, network, and get inspired to take action in the coming year.

**Stay tuned for more details and RSVP information!**

[Gabriela Garcia](https://www.thetpw.org/)
