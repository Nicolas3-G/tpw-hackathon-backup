# In-District Advocacy

Dec 13

Written by [Gabriela Garcia](https://www.thetpw.org/)

**TIME SENSITIVE: In-district advocacy**

We are organizing district meetings and need your support in key districts. Your voice is vital to demonstrate the impact and success of rehabilitative programming.

If you haven’t already, please register at [TPW User Registration](https://forms.monday.com/forms/6fe07096cd67f87517197f6d09dc75e1?r=use1),  or email us at [advocacy@thetpw.org](mailto:advocacy@thetpw.org) and copy [membership@thetpw.org](mailto:membership@thetpw.org) with the names of your district representatives to ensure we can invite you to join us in lobbying efforts. You can use this [Find your State Representative](https://findyourrep.legislature.ca.gov/) tool to identify your representatives. 

Your participation is essential in driving this important work forward. Thank you for your support!

[Gabriela Garcia](https://www.thetpw.org/)
