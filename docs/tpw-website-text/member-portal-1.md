## Advocacy Initiatives

- 

  #### AB 1104 - Passed

  The legislation was revised to state that the deprivation of liberty satisfies the punitive purpose of sentencing and that the purpose of incarceration is rehabilitation and successful community reintegration through education, treatment, and active participation in rehabilitative and restorative justice programs.

- #### AB 581 - Passed

  TPW co-sponsored AB 581 (Carrillo)—the *Providing Access to Transformation and Healing (PATH) Act*—alongside GRIP Training Institute and Initiate Justice. This bill, now signed into law by Governor Newsom, ensures that Community-Based Organization (CBO) Program Providers doing the essential work of rehabilitation and restoration inside CDCR facilities have a more streamlined and accessible clearance process.

  We understand this will be a gradual shift, as these policies are brand new. To support you in this process:

  - Please see the \[CDCR Memo page\] for the latest info on clearance procedures.

  - Help us track progress by completing the \[[AB 581 Clearance Reporting Form](https://forms.gle/2wm84hEGvqTtbXfn9)\]

- #### RIGHT Grant 1.0 - Funded

  TPW co-authored the Rehabilitative Investment Grant for Healing and Transformation (RIGHT) in partnership with CDCR. The RIGHT Grant is a non-competitive grant specifically designed to support transformative, rehabilitative programs inside CDCR facilities. Each year, TPW actively lobbies and advocates to secure continued funding for this critical grant, ensuring that these life-changing programs can thrive.

  ## Updated AB 581 Memo

  This memo states: "As CDCR works to update the Department Operational Manuel (DOM) for the new process some of the forms in the standardized Program Provider packet will be updated too. The current 966 form asks persons to identify if they have ever been on parole and since discharged. If yes, they are directed to draft a letter to the warden explaining. With the implementation of this process that letter/memorandum will no longer be required, no program provider will be denied the ability to enter an institution for not drafting and or submitting this letter. The CDCR Form 966 will be updated to remove this verbiage later as part of the DOM updates."

- #### Join, Change, or Leave TPW Google Email Group \[ListServ\]

  To join the TPW Google email group:

  1.  Go to <https://groups.google.com/g/_tpw> and select where it says, for access try “joining the group.”

  2.  You will have an option to sync your profile if you are a gmail user, which means everyone in the group can see your profile.

  3.  Next you can choose what type of subscription you want from getting a email for every message posted, getting daly summaries (where up to 25 complete messages are combined into single emails and sent daily), combined updates (where summaries of up to 150 messages are combined into a single email sent daily), or not to get email updates which means you can only see the messages if you go to the Google group page.

  To change you subscription:

  1.  Go to <https://groups.google.com/my-groups> and look for a column titled “Subscription” and look for the drop down menu in the row for \_tpw@googlegroups.com (in case you are a subscriber of more than one group).

  2.  Change the drop down menu to your preferred choice.

  To LEAVE the group:

  1.  Go to <https://groups.google.com/my-groups> and look for the left facing arrow icon with a line on the right side of the line for \_tpw@googlegroups.com.

  Note: You can also go to <https://groups.google.com/my-groups> and tick the star icon next to any group to add it to your favorite’s list.
