  ## Arts in Corrections Funding Model Survey

  This survey is exploratory. Its purpose is to:

  - Understand how a potential shift to a grant model might affect current AIC awardees

  - Gather perspectives on risks, benefits, and unintended consequences

  - Identify what protections, language, or structures would be necessary if changes were considered

  - Assess interest in collective discussion and coordination

  This survey does not assume that a change should or will happen. The information gathered will be used to inform conversations and determine whether there is shared interest, concern, or alignment around future AIC funding models.

  [Click Here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScVxMgfqJ-qqnsmGdLogUbCmq3MJJuKMwzm6rwi1sbmZ2AlfQ/viewform?usp=dialog)
