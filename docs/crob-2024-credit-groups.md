# The 2024 list of credit-earning groups: it exists, but we're not using it

Checked 24 October 2026. **Decision: too old to pre-fill the form with. Don't build on it.**

## What it is

The California Rehabilitation Oversight Board's [2024 annual report](https://crob.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/2024-C-ROB-Annual-Report.pdf) has an appendix (Appendix D, report pages 71 to 77) listing every group approved to earn sentence-reduction credit (Rehabilitative Achievement Credit) at 32 prisons. It covers volunteer-run groups as well as grant-funded ones, so it's much wider than CDCR's grant list in `public-data-sources.md`.

It has group names and prisons only. No yards, dates, participant counts or hours.

40 organisations on TPW's member list appear in it, 26 of them not on CDCR's grant list. That's why it was tempting.

## Why we're not using it

- **It's from June 2024,** more than two years old.
- **There won't be a newer one.** The board was abolished by AB 134 (signed 27 June 2025), so the 2024 report was its last. The board's website no longer loads. The copy we read came from the Internet Archive.
- **The list changes a lot from year to year.** The same board counted about 1,300 activity groups in June 2022 ([2022 report](https://crob.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/2022-C-ROB-Report.pdf)) and about 856 in June 2024. It doesn't say whether groups closed or the counting changed. Either way, a fair share of the 2024 entries are likely out of date.
- **Nothing newer of its kind is public.** CDCR's [activity groups page](https://www.cdcr.ca.gov/community-partners/inmate-activity-groups) lists only types of group, not which groups run where.

The most recent public list of who runs what at which prison is CDCR's grant list (as of 1 January 2026, see `public-data-sources.md`). It only covers grant-funded programmes.

## If we ever want a current version

Under the credit rule ([Title 15 §3043.4(f)](https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/15-CCR-3043.4)), each prison's warden has to publish a list of approved credit-earning activities and update it every year. Those lists aren't online. Getting them would mean asking CDCR, for example through a Public Records Act request on [CDCR's portal](https://californiacdcr.govqa.us/). That's TPW's call, not ours, because members worry about CDCR pushback.

Richie has the 2024 list as a spreadsheet (846 rows, matched to TPW members) if anyone needs it for reference.
