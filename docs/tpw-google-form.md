# TPW's current Google Form: "TPW - Collective Impact Data"

This is the Google Form TPW sends to member organizations today. It was rebuilt on 2026-10-24 from screenshots of a screen recording of the live form, because we don't have the form's own link. None of the questions appeared to be marked as required, and the form does not collect respondents' email addresses.

## How the form differs from the Data Entry Sheet

- The form already uses the label "Course Hours (per Participant)", the rename the June 2026 glossary proposes. The Data Entry Sheet tab still says "Total Programming hours".
- The form has no "Funded by RIGHT 2.0" question, no calculated columns and no Notes field.
- The Form Responses tab in the snapshot holds one response, dated 2026-07-22. The 37 real cycles are in the Data Entry Sheet tab.
